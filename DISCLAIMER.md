# Disclaimer

This is a personally owned iPhone 6s. I bought it used, and it still had
the previous owner's iCloud activation lock on it. Everything here is from
jailbreaking and locally bypassing the activation screen on that one
device, for my own use.

This repo is not a tool. There is no binary, no script you run to unlock a
phone - just write-ups, two bug reports filed against palera1n/jbinit, and
two small C files for talking to the PongoOS console. ECID, UDID, serial
number, account info - all stripped out of everything here.

Activation lock exists so stolen or lost phones cannot just get wiped and
resold. What is in `docs/walkthrough.md` is a local bypass of the
activation screen on a device I physically have - it does not touch Apple's
servers, does not recover or extract any account keys, and does not restore
iCloud, Find My, or carrier service on the device. If you do this on a
phone that is not yours, that is on you - legally and otherwise. I'm not
going to pretend this could not be misused, but I did not build it to be
used that way, and I am not responsible for what someone else does with it.

No warranty, obviously. Use your own judgment.
